ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding our shared heritage.

A university web development project exploring UNESCO World Heritage Sites around the world.

— PURPOSE

Connecting people with places that matter.

The UNESCO Heritage Explorer is designed to provide an accessible and engaging way to discover important cultural and natural heritage sites around the world.

Each place tells a different story about human civilization, architecture, history, culture, or the natural environment.
